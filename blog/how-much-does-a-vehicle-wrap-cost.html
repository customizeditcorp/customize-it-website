By Customize It · Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

The most common question we get from business owners on the Central Coast is: "How much does a vehicle wrap cost?" The honest answer is that it depends — but this guide gives you real numbers from our Santa Maria shop so you can budget without surprises.

What Affects the Price of a Vehicle Wrap?

1. Vehicle Size

The biggest driver of cost is the square footage of vinyl required. A Prius takes far less material and labor than a Ford F-350 crew cab dually. When quoting, we always ask for the make, model, and year — body style matters too (crew cab vs. regular cab, extended vs. standard wheelbase).

2. Coverage Area

A partial wrap covering only the doors and rear costs significantly less than a full wrap that covers every panel including the roof. Many contractors on the Central Coast go with partial wraps on work trucks — logo, name, and phone on both doors — and get 80% of the brand impact at half the price.

3. Design Complexity

A simple two-color layout with your logo and contact info is quick to design and print. A full-color photographic wrap with gradients, cutouts, and multiple graphic elements takes more design time and specialized printing. We offer graphic design in-house so you are not paying a separate agency.

4. Material Grade

We use cast vinyl (3M 1080 series or Avery Supreme Wrapping Film) for all vehicle wraps. Cast vinyl conforms to curves and complex body lines, resists shrinkage, and lasts 5–7 years. Calendered vinyl is cheaper but cracks faster and is harder to remove cleanly — we do not use it for vehicle wraps.

5. Number of Vehicles

Fleet orders of 3 or more vehicles receive a 10–15% discount on installation because setup and design costs are spread across the order. If you run a landscape crew, plumbing company, or cleaning service with multiple vehicles, call us for a fleet quote.

Is a Vehicle Wrap Worth the Money?

A wrapped work truck generates an estimated 30,000–70,000 impressions per day in a typical market. At a cost of $3,500–$5,000 for a full wrap lasting 5 years, that works out to less than $0.01 per impression — cheaper than virtually any other form of advertising. For a local service business where trust and name recognition drive calls, it is one of the highest-ROI marketing investments available.

At Customize It, our wraps pay for themselves within the first year for most of our clients. RM Sanitation, Lorenzo's Plumbing, and Luna & Sons Landscaping all came back to wrap additional vehicles after seeing results from their first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a full vehicle wrap cost?
A full wrap on a standard car or van runs $2,500–$4,500. Larger vehicles (box trucks, trailers) run $5,000–$10,000+. Price depends on vehicle size, design complexity, and material. All our wraps use 3M or Avery Dennison vinyl with a 5-year warranty.
How much does a partial vehicle wrap cost?
Partial wraps covering doors, hood, or rear panels run $800–$2,000. They offer 70–80% of the brand impact of a full wrap at roughly half the cost — a popular choice for contractors and service businesses.
What is the cheapest way to brand a work truck?
Removable magnetic signs start at $200 for a pair. They are great for vehicles you share for personal and business use. Permanent vinyl door decals are the next step up at $300–$600 per vehicle.
How long does a vehicle wrap last?
A quality cast vinyl wrap lasts 5–7 years with proper care. Hand wash or use a touchless car wash. Avoid high-pressure washing pointed directly at edges. We back our wraps with a 5-year warranty on materials and installation.
Does a vehicle wrap damage the paint?
No — premium cast vinyl actually protects the original paint from UV, minor scratches, and road debris. When properly installed and professionally removed, it leaves the original paint in better condition than before. Cheap film or DIY removal can cause issues.
