Frequently Asked Questions About Vehicle Wraps in Santa Maria, CA

We answer the most common questions before you even have to ask.

How much does a vehicle wrap cost in Santa Maria?
At Customize It, prices start at $200 for removable magnets, $300 for vinyl decals, $800 for partial wraps, and $2,500–$5,000+ for full commercial wraps using 3M or Avery Dennison materials. The quote is free — call (805) 345-2773.
How long does a vehicle wrap with 3M materials last?
With 3M and Avery Dennison materials, a well-maintained wrap lasts between 5 and 7 years. All our wraps include a 5-year warranty on materials and installation.
Does the wrap damage the original paint?
No — on the contrary, it protects it. The vinyl acts as a protective layer. When removed correctly, the paint is in perfect condition, preserving resale value.
How long does installation take?
A full commercial wrap takes 2–3 days at our Santa Maria shop. Partial wraps and simple lettering can be ready in 1 day. We offer express service based on availability.
Can I wash the vehicle normally?
Yes. Hand washing or touchless automatic washing are safe. We recommend avoiding high-pressure washers too close to the vinyl to extend the life of the wrap.
Do you offer fleet discounts?
Yes. We offer special pricing for fleets of 3 or more vehicles. We've wrapped complete fleets for plumbing, landscaping, construction, and cleaning companies on the Central Coast. Call (805) 345-2773.
Do I need to have my logo ready to get started?
No. If you don't have a logo, our graphic design team creates it as part of the project. We are a complete solution: design, printing, and installation in Santa Maria, CA.
What types of vehicles can you wrap?
We wrap all types of commercial vehicles: trucks, vans, trailers, pickups, food trucks, and complete fleets. If it has wheels and runs on the Central Coast, we'll wrap it.
